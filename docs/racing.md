# Racing

The **Racers** card puts up to three opponents on the course with you, compared at
the same elapsed time:

1. **Your best**: with a goal, the saved row that reached that distance fastest;
   on an open row, the saved row (500 m or longer) with the best average pace.
2. **Your most recent rows**.
3. **Pacers**: virtual boats at a fixed pace, filling any empty slots. Set their
   pace with the slider on the card; the three pacers sit around it.

Each racer shows how far ahead or behind you are, in seconds.

## The course

With a goal the course runs from 0 to that distance. On an open row it is a little
longer than whoever is furthest along, so everyone stays on screen.

In fullscreen the course is a vertical bar: start at the bottom, finish at the top,
a dot per racer and a larger one for you. Drag it anywhere, or switch it off under
**More → Race bar in fullscreen**.

## Racing other people

Not supported. Live racing against other rowers needs a server to relay everyone's
position, and Fellwater deliberately has no backend.
